The platform concept

Slate adaptability. Evergreen Auto mission configuration.

Slate describes its vehicle as a customizable electric pickup with multiple accessory and body configurations. Evergreen Auto's concept is to evaluate how a production vehicle platform could be adapted for non-weaponized base operations and field-support duties.

  • Installation and facilities support
  • Logistics, transport, and utility tasks
  • Maintenance and service support
